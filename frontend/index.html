ENTERPRISE STRATEGIC ROADMAP

Hospital Scalability & Integration Architecture

Engineering roadmap to transition Gerta AI from a single-clinic voice coordinator prototype to a federated, high-availability healthcare enterprise platform.

Enterprise Horizon: 4 Phases

1. Bi-Directional EHR Interoperability

Synchronizes voice registrations with Epic Systems, Oracle Cerner, and AthenaHealth using HL7 FHIR R4 standards and SMART-on-FHIR OAuth 2.0.

Spec: HL7 FHIR v4.0.1 • mTLS Auth

2. Clinic Calendar & Slot Booking

Extends intake to offer and lock appointment slots via provider calendar APIs, with automated Twilio SMS booking reminders and CalDAV sync.

Spec: Epic Cadence • CalDAV / iCal • Twilio SMS

3. Real-Time Payer Eligibility

Sub-second EDI X12 270/271 verification against Change Healthcare, Availity, or Stedi to inform callers of real-time co-pays and deductibles.

Spec: ANSI ASC X12 270/271 • Clearinghouse API

4. Multilingual Healthcare Equity

Zero-lag acoustic language identification switching between English, Spanish, and French with native neural TTS voices to satisfy Title VI Civil Rights standards.

Spec: Deepgram Multilingual • Cartesia Neural Sonic

5. HIPAA KMS Envelope Encryption

Hardware Security Module key management with AES-256-GCM envelope encryption protecting individual PHI fields to satisfy HIPAA Rule § 164.312(a)(2)(iv).

Spec: AWS KMS / Vault • AES-256-GCM • SHA-256 Chain

PHASE 2 • INTEROPERABILITY & HOSPITAL INTEGRATION

Bi-Directional HL7 FHIR R4 EHR Bridge

Direct synchronization between Gerta AI and hospital Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems such as Epic Systems, Oracle Cerner, and AthenaHealth using the HL7 FHIR R4 standard.

HL7 FHIR v4.0.1 • SMART-on-FHIR

What it is

An asynchronous microservice converting Pydantic patient records into standardized FHIR Patient resources and publishing them via SMART-on-FHIR OAuth 2.0 endpoints.

What it can be used for

Completely eliminates duplicate manual entry by medical receptionists, automatically creating verified patient charts in hospital EHRs before the caller's first appointment.

How it operates

Upon phone call completion, an event-driven worker maps demographics to FHIR JSON and dispatches a signed POST /fhir/R4/Patient request over mutual TLS (mTLS).

EDI X12 270/271 • Change Healthcare / Availity

What it is

Direct API connector to Change Healthcare, Availity, or Stedi for automated real-time patient insurance policy verification.

What it can be used for

Alerts the patient immediately if their policy is inactive, requires pre-authorization, or has an outstanding deductible before they arrive at the clinic.

How it operates

When the caller provides insurance provider and member ID, Sarah triggers verify_eligibility; if rejected, she gently asks for secondary insurance or informs them of self-pay options.

What it is

AES-256-GCM column-level envelope encryption managed via AWS KMS or HashiCorp Vault for dates of birth, phone numbers, and member IDs.
